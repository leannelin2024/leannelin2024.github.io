Background

LeAnne Lin is a performer based in New York City. She is originally from the suburbs of Illinois, where she got her start performing in local choirs, musicals, and plays.

Training

Following her passion, LeAnne attended Interlochen Arts Academy as a theater major. She continued her studies at NYU, where she sang in the New Student Seminar and performed with the Broadway Orchestra, directed by the notable musical director Ted Sperling, in both the Spring and Fall of 2023.

Notable Performances

  • 2023NYU Broadway Orchestra - Spring & Fall, dir. Ted Sperling
  • 2019Featured soloist - Interlochen Arts Academy's Collage
  • 2018Semi-finalist - Classical Voice Competition
  • 2017Solo performance - Carnegie Hall
  • 2016Solo performance - Carnegie Hall
LeAnne is excited to bring her talent and passion to this industry!
