LeAnne Lin

Singer / Actress / Performer

LeAnne Lin is a performer based in New York City. She is originally from the suburbs of Illinois. She got her start performing in local choirs, musicals, and plays. She followed her passion and attended Interlochen Arts Academy as a theater major.

Some notable performances include two solo performances at Carnegie Hall in 2016 and 2017, respectively, semi-finalist in the Classical Voice Competition in 2018, and featured soloist at Interlochen Arts Academy's Collage in 2019. At NYU, she sang in the New Student Seminar and performed with the Broadway Orchestra, directed by the notable musical director Ted Sperling, in both the Spring and Fall of 2023.

LeAnne is excited to bring her talent and passion to this industry!
